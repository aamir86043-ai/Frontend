What we collect

Your name and username, a random user ID, and a sign-in token stored in your browser so we can recognise you without a password. When you submit a group, we store the group details you enter. When you report a group, we store your report text.

Why we store your name and username

To give you a profile, to show who submitted a group, and to keep usernames unique.

Group-link clicks

When you press Join and confirm, we save your user ID, the group ID, the event type and the time before opening the invite link. We use this to count how often groups are opened.
